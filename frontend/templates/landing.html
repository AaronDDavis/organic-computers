Understanding PCOS

A common condition,
frequently undiagnosed.

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome is a hormonal disorder affecting people with ovaries of reproductive age. It disrupts the normal functioning of the ovaries and can have wide-ranging effects on metabolic, reproductive, and psychological health.
